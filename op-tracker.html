How It Works
Each exercise card has two rows. Here's what they mean:
LAST ROW (grey)
Auto-populated from your most recent session for that exercise. This is what you need to beat.
THIS ROW (cyan)
What you actually did today. Fill this in during your session. Data saves automatically.
COMPLETE SESSION
Tap when done. This saves everything permanently and shifts This → Last for next time.

RPE Reference
RPE 10Muscular failure — could not complete another rep
RPE 9One rep remaining — target for all working sets
RPE 8Two reps remaining
RPE 7Three reps remaining
RPE 6–7Deload intensity — use when deload week is triggered only
Progression Rules
Rep increment+1 rep per working set. Keep load fixed until ceiling is hit
Load increaseAll sets at rep ceiling × 2 consecutive sessions
Load amountSmallest available increment (e.g. 2.5 kg each side)
StallNo rep improvement × 2 consecutive sessions — check RPE first
Deload triggerNo progression × 3+ consecutive weeks at RPE 9
Deload protocol50% of working sets at RPE 6–7. Return at full load next week
Data Backup

Export regularly. Import to restore on a new device.

Data & Privacy

All data is stored locally on this device only. Optimal Protocol cannot access, view, or recover your data. Export regularly to prevent loss if your browser cache is cleared. Optimal Protocol accepts no liability for data loss.
