Volume Landmarks

Weekly direct working sets per muscle. MEV is the minimum for growth, MAV the productive range, MRV the most you can recover from. Indirect volume is shown in Insights but never counts toward these.

RPE Reference
RPE 10Muscular failure — could not complete another rep
RPE 9One rep remaining — target for all working sets
RPE 8Two reps remaining
RPE 7Three reps remaining
RPE 6–7Deload intensity — use when deload week is triggered only
Progression Rules
Rep increment+1 rep per working set. Keep load fixed until ceiling is hit
Load increaseAll sets at rep ceiling × 2 consecutive sessions
Load amountSmallest available increment (e.g. 2.5 kg each side)
StallNo load or rep improvement × 5 consecutive sessions at RPE 9+
Deload triggerNo progression × 5 consecutive sessions at RPE 9+
Deload protocol50% of working sets at RPE 6–7. Return at full load next week
Data Backup

Export regularly. Import to restore on a new device.

Data & Privacy

All data is stored locally on this device only. Optimal Protocol cannot access, view, or recover your data. Export regularly to prevent loss if your browser cache is cleared. Optimal Protocol accepts no liability for data loss.

Before you start
Add to your Home Screen
Your training data is stored on this device, not on a server. That keeps it private and free forever — but it means browsers delete it after about a week of not opening the app. Installing to your Home Screen protects it permanently.
ON IPHONE / IPAD
1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari
2. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen
3. Tap Add
4. Open OP Tracker from your new icon
ON ANDROID
1. Tap the ⋮ menu in Chrome
2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen
3. Confirm, then open from your new icon
You're on a desktop browser. The tracker works here, but it's built for phones — open it on your phone and install it there for the best experience.
If you continue in the browser, export a backup regularly from Settings.
